# OpenRPG

**Pitch:** A handheld top-down adventure: explore a town, enter dungeons, fight, and take down a generated boss.

**Core loop (30s):** Move with the joystick, press A to talk or strike, clear a room, go deeper.

**Three mechanics:**
1. Explore the overworld on a grid and talk or interact with A.
2. Enter a room, read threats, fight with A, leave when it is clear.
3. Descend a generated dungeon and face a generated boss at the end.

**Art direction (one phrase):** Pixel art, 16x16 tiles, bird's-eye

**Scope:** Vertical slice

**Win condition:** Reach the bottom of the first dungeon and defeat its boss.

**One thing this is NOT:** A mouse-driven or keyboard-menu game — no pointer, text entry, or fourth button; joystick plus A/B/C only.

**Inspirations:** The Legend of Zelda (NES overworld), Link's Awakening (handheld dungeons), Cadence of Hyrule (tight grid combat), Binding of Isaac (generated floors, parked as later content density).

**Parked for later:** Full dynamic boss/dungeon generation beyond one slice floor; inventory crafting; side quests; B/C combat verbs until the A-button loop is solid.

# Open Meadow

**Pitch:** A handheld real-time meadow: walk four colored lands, swing or spin
with A, pause with B, and hunt hearts and boosters while brutes and mages
close in.

**Core loop (30s):** Move with the joystick, tap A to slash, hold A to charge
a spin, stay alive with five hearts, grab a pink/gold/blue pickup if you need
it.

**Three mechanics:**
1. Free eight-way movement across Iceland, Fireland, Purpleland, and Grassland,
   with a north-up radar.
2. Combat: tap A for a short slash, hold A then release for a radial spin;
   brutes melee, mages shoot fireballs.
3. Survival pickups: hearts heal or add a sixth heart; gold power hits harder;
   blue shield blocks a hit.

**Art direction (one phrase):** Retro handheld meadow — simple shapes, pixel
hearts, outlined menu type.

**Scope:** Vertical slice on Uno Q (960×540, joystick + A/B/C).

**Win condition:** Stay on your feet and clear or outlast the meadow pack;
there is no dungeon boss in this slice.

**One thing this is NOT:** A mouse-driven or keyboard-menu game — no pointer,
text entry, or fourth button; joystick plus A/B/C only.

**Inspirations:** NES Zelda overworld feel, handheld pause menus, simple
pickup hearts and timed power-ups.

**Parked for later:** Generated dungeons/bosses, inventory crafting, extra
combat verbs on C, audio layer, board playtest sign-off for the latest
pickups and menu.
